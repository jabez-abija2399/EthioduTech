# PRODUCT RULES — PERMANENT GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES

These principles are permanent and non-negotiable. They govern all product decisions, architectural choices, curriculum engineering, UI/UX designs, and code implementations for the **Edutech Platform**.

---

## 1. Core Educational Principles

1. **Learning > Engagement**
   - The primary purpose of the platform is educational growth and mastery, not maximizing screen time or vanity engagement metrics.

2. **Mastery > Completion**
   - Progressing through a course requires demonstrating actual capability and understanding, not merely clicking "Mark Complete" or watching a video.

3. **Creation > Consumption**
   - Students learn by creating, coding, building, and solving real problems—not by passively consuming media.

4. **Student Thinking > AI Answers**
   - AI serves as a Socratic guide, tutor, and debugger. AI must never directly generate complete assignment solutions for students or substitute student critical thinking.

5. **Projects > Certificates**
   - Real, functional web projects published to a verifiable student portfolio are the ultimate proof of learning, far superior to unverified digital certificates.

6. **Evidence > Assumptions**
   - Educational strategies, UX patterns, and learning models must be validated by empirical student outcomes and empirical software testing.

---

## 2. Safety & Operational Principles

7. **Safety > Growth**
   - Child safety, privacy protection, data minimization, and secure online interactions take absolute precedence over aggressive platform growth strategies.

8. **Accessibility > Visual Complexity**
   - Simple, clean, fast-loading, accessible, and high-contrast user interfaces are prioritized over complex, resource-heavy visual animations.

9. **Quality > Speed**
   - Software must be correct, fully tested, secure, and resilient before release. Superficial quick fixes and fake completion are strictly prohibited.

10. **Simplicity > Unnecessary Features**
    - Avoid bloat and scope creep. Implement strictly what is required for the current product release stage.

11. **Real Learning > Vanity Metrics**
    - Measure verified skill acquisition, problem-solving independence, and project quality rather than arbitrary daily active user spikes.

12. **Independent Problem Solving > AI Dependence**
    - The learning experience must develop student autonomy. Assistance provided by the system should decrease as student mastery increases.

13. **Reusable Architecture > Duplicated Implementation**
    - Codebases must follow clean architectural boundaries, modular design systems, clean data access patterns, and scalable standards.

---

## 3. Decision Framework

Whenever a technical, UI, or product conflict arises, evaluate the options against these rules in order:
1. Does it protect student safety and privacy?
2. Does it improve real learning and creation?
3. Does it work under low-bandwidth / offline conditions?
4. Is it maintainable, performant, and simple?
