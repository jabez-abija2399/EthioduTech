# Feedback and Hint System

Feedback must help the learner continue learning. Avoid simple "Correct!" or "Wrong."
Every feedback response must include: What happened, Why it happened, What principle applies, and What to try next.

## Hint Ladder
Level 0: Independent attempt
Level 1: Question
Level 2: Concept reminder
Level 3: Strategic hint
Level 4: Partial example
Level 5: Detailed walkthrough
Level 6: Full solution (when justified)
