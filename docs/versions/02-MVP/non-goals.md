# MVP Non-Goals

To prevent scope creep and ensure we deliver a focused, effective learning tool, the following features are explicitly **EXCLUDED** from the MVP phase.

## Excluded Features
1. **Course Marketplace:** We are not building a marketplace with hundreds of courses. The MVP features only one course.
2. **Complex Recommendation Engine:** We do not need AI to recommend "what to watch next."
3. **Advanced Social Network:** There will be no news feeds, unrestricted direct messaging, or friend requests.
4. **Full Global Payments:** We will focus on either free beta access or simple, local payment gateways (e.g., Telebirr) rather than complex global subscription logic.
5. **Extensive Language Support:** We are not supporting Python, C++, Rust, etc. The MVP focuses strictly on Web Technologies (HTML/CSS/JS).
6. **Advanced AI Agents:** The AI tutor will use basic prompting and context injection; we are not building autonomous grading agents or multi-agent swarms.
7. **Complicated Certificates:** We focus on the portfolio. PDF certificates with blockchain verification, etc., are excluded.
8. **Enterprise LMS Features:** No complex school district onboarding, bulk licensing flows, or massive administrative hierarchies.
9. **Live Classes:** No real-time video streaming or massive live-class infrastructure.
