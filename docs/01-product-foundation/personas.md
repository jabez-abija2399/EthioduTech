# User Personas

## 1. Student 10–12
**Profile**: Curious, highly energetic, easily distracted.
**Tech Literacy**: Comfortable with smartphones and basic apps, but no coding experience.
**Needs**: Engaging, visual, and highly interactive learning. Frequent positive reinforcement.
**Challenges**: Short attention span; easily frustrated by complex text or non-obvious UI.
**Safety**: Requires maximum safety controls. No direct messaging; parent-managed account.

## 2. Student 13–15
**Profile**: Seeking independence, interested in building things to show peers.
**Tech Literacy**: High mobile literacy; some may have touched basic HTML or school computer labs.
**Needs**: Real-world context (why am I learning this?). Ability to build projects they can share.
**Challenges**: Susceptible to giving up if they don't see immediate, cool results. 
**Safety**: Needs moderate safety controls and guided collaboration.

## 3. Student 16–18
**Profile**: Thinking about university, career, or side income. Highly motivated by outcomes.
**Tech Literacy**: May have some coding experience; understands the value of digital skills.
**Needs**: Professional tools (real IDE, GitHub), challenging projects, and portfolio building to prove their skills to others.
**Challenges**: Balancing platform learning with school exams. Needs efficient, high-impact learning.
**Safety**: Needs professional collaboration tools; standard safety and moderation.

## 4. Parent
**Profile**: Wants the best future for their child; may or may not be tech-savvy.
**Needs**: To see tangible progress. Wants to know their money is well spent and their child is safe.
**Challenges**: Might not understand the code their child writes, so they need simple progress reports and portfolio views.

## 5. Teacher
**Profile**: May be a school teacher using the platform in a classroom setting.
**Needs**: Dashboards to track student progress, identify who is stuck, and manage assignments.
**Challenges**: Large class sizes; limited time to give individual attention. Needs the platform to handle the heavy lifting of grading and basic tutoring.

## 6. Course Instructor
**Profile**: Subject matter expert creating content.
**Needs**: Tools to build interactive lessons, define projects, and set AI tutor parameters.
**Challenges**: Translating complex knowledge into beginner-friendly, interactive formats.

## 7. School
**Profile**: Educational institution adopting the platform for its students.
**Needs**: Bulk licensing, administrative oversight, curriculum alignment, and reporting.
**Challenges**: Budget constraints and integration with existing school schedules.

## 8. Platform Administrator
**Profile**: Oversees the health, safety, and performance of the platform.
**Needs**: Moderation tools, user management, analytics dashboards, and system alerts.
**Challenges**: Ensuring safety at scale and maintaining platform uptime in a low-bandwidth environment.
