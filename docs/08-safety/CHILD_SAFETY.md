# CHILD SAFETY & MINOR PROTECTION POLICY

## 1. Safety Principles for Grades 5–12
1. **No Unrestricted Social Messaging:** Direct messaging between users is omitted in MVP to eliminate grooming or cyberbullying vectors.
2. **Minimization of Public Information:** Public portfolios (`/portfolio/[studentId]`) render project artifacts without disclosing personal contact numbers, physical addresses, or full real names by default.
3. **Parental & Teacher Oversight:** Parents (`/parent`) and Teachers (`/teacher`) have visibility into student progress and submitted reflections.
4. **Content Moderation:** AI tutor prompts filter inappropriate language or unsafe input strings.
