# The Learning Model

## Core Philosophy
The platform must develop capable, creative, and independent problem solvers. The primary metric of success is **Student Capability** (what they can actually do), not the number of videos watched or certificates earned.

## The Core Learning Loop
Every major course feature and lesson must connect to this foundational loop:

1. **Diagnostic:** Assess what the student currently knows and identify gaps before learning begins.
2. **Lesson:** Provide a concise, engaging explanation of the concept (Why this matters → Concept → Simple explanation).
3. **Example:** Demonstrate the concept in action with a clear, interactive, or step-by-step example.
4. **Practice:** Guided and independent practice to reinforce the concept immediately after the lesson.
5. **Challenge:** Present a small problem that requires the student to apply the concept without step-by-step guidance.
6. **Build:** Integrate the concept into a larger project or mini-project.
7. **Submit:** The student submits their work or code for review.
8. **Feedback:** AI or peer review provides targeted feedback (identifying misconceptions, giving hints).
9. **Reflection:** The student is prompted to explain what they did and why it worked, solidifying understanding.
10. **Mastery:** Assess if the student has truly mastered the skill before allowing them to advance.
11. **Portfolio:** The completed project or significant milestone is added to the student's public body of work.

## AI as a Learning Assistant
AI on this platform is not designed to do the work for the student. It functions as a strict but helpful tutor.
- **Level 1 (Question):** "What are you trying to achieve?"
- **Level 2 (Concept Hint):** Reminds the student of the relevant concept.
- **Level 3 (Direction):** Points the student towards the specific line or logic error.
- **Level 4 (Partial Example):** Provides a generic example similar to the problem.
- **Level 5 (Detailed Explanation):** Breaks down why the student's approach is failing.
- **Level 6 (Solution Review):** Only after multiple attempts, reviews the correct solution with the student.

The AI will actively combat "cheating" by detecting when a student is overly reliant on it and pivoting back to the Socratic method (asking questions).
